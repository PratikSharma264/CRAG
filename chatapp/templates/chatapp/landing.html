{# Q2: How retrieval works #}

How does it find the answer?

Your question is encoded with MedCPT, a biomedical retrieval model trained on PubMed — not general web text. It finds the most relevant passages in the knowledge base, then a language model synthesizes them into a grounded answer. Every passage used is cited.

{# Q3: Sources & KB #}

Where do the sources come from?

A curated collection of peer-reviewed abstracts on pediatric diabetes — covering CGM, insulin pump therapy, DKA prevention, glycemic targets, psychosocial outcomes, and technology adoption. You can browse the entire knowledge base yourself.

Browse knowledge base
{# Q4: Trust signal #}

What if there's no evidence?

The system says so. If the knowledge base doesn't cover your question, it refuses to answer rather than guess. No hallucinations, no fabricated citations. What you see is exactly what the evidence supports — nothing more.
