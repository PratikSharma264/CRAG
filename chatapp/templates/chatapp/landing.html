How it works

From clinical question to cited evidence

1

Ask in clinical language

Type a question like you'd ask a colleague: "What's the evidence for CGM in adolescents with T1D?"

2

Retrieve with MedCPT

A biomedical retrieval model built for scientific text finds the most relevant passages in the knowledge base.

3

Read a grounded answer

The answer is synthesized from retrieved passages only. If evidence is missing, the app says so — no hallucinations.

Why it matters

Built for evidence-based practice

Biomedical retrieval

Questions and abstracts matched with MedCPT encoders — a model trained on PubMed, not general web text.

Sources on every answer

Each response lists the abstracts and passages used, with a direct link to the knowledge base entry.

Transparent knowledge base

Browse every paper the system can draw from. Read-only — what you see is exactly what answers are based on.

No fabrication

If the knowledge base doesn't cover your question, the system refuses to answer rather than guess.

Current scope

Focused on pediatric diabetes

The knowledge base currently covers research on type 1 and type 2 diabetes in children and adolescents — including CGM, insulin pump therapy, DKA prevention, glycemic targets, and psychosocial outcomes.

  • Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM)
  • Insulin pump therapy
  • Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA)
  • Glycemic targets & HbA1c
  • Psychosocial & quality of life
  • Technology adoption & adherence
